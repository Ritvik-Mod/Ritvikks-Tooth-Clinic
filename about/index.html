About · Dr. Deepika Mod · Microendodontist

Twenty-five years
of looking closer.

A specialist, Microendodontist and Professor, Dr. Deepika has spent her entire career proving that the highest standard of dentistry is achieved through microscopic precision and a patient-centred approach.

Philosophy

The right treatment, not
the biggest one.

Dr. Deepika's practice is built on three principles: precision tooling, honest diagnosis, and the patience to do small work well so big work is rarely needed.

01 · See first

Diagnose under magnification.

Magnification-assisted examination reveals what radiographs alone can miss — small fractures, hidden canals, early caries. We treat what's actually there.

02 · Explain second

Options on paper before any treatment.

Diagnosis, alternatives, costs and treatment plan are written down before treatment begins. You leave the first visit with a plan, not a sales pitch.

03 · Treat last

Conserve tooth structure wherever possible.

The most successful long-term result is the one that preserves the most of your natural tooth. Conservative, predictable, lasting.

Dr. Deepika Mod · In her own words

"Specialist care isn't a luxury.
It's the work done well, the first time."

Over the years, my patients have taught me one thing — the kindest thing a dentist can do is finish a treatment so well that you don't have to think about it again.

Why a specialist matters

Why microendodontics?

A microendodontist does what a general dentist trains for in a few weeks — every working day, under magnification. The difference shows up in first-time success rates, in fewer retreatments, and in how rarely a tooth needs to be extracted later.
