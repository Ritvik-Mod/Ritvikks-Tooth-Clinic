Procedure · Dentures

Dentures

Custom-fitted removable replacements for missing teeth — comfortable, functional, and natural-looking.

Specialty Prosthetic
Duration Typically 4–5 visits over 2–4 weeks
Pain expectation No pain during fabrication. Some initial soreness as you adjust to the denture.
Specialist Dr. Deepika Mod

When you might need this

  • Multiple missing teeth in a row
  • All teeth missing (complete dentures)
  • When implants are not feasible or preferred
  • Replacement of old, ill-fitting dentures

What the procedure involves

  1. Examination and impressions of remaining teeth and gums
  2. Bite registration and aesthetic trial
  3. Try-in of the wax setup to confirm fit and look
  4. Delivery of the final denture with follow-up adjustments

How long it takes

Typically 4–5 visits over 2–4 weeks.

Pain expectation

No pain during fabrication. Some initial soreness as you adjust to the denture — adjustments are made at follow-up visits.

Technology used

  • High-quality acrylic and metal frameworks
  • Aesthetic tooth setups
  • Precision impression techniques

Recovery & aftercare

A few weeks of adjustment is normal. Soft foods initially. We will guide you through cleaning and care.
