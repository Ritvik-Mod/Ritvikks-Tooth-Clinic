Procedure · Teeth Whitening

Teeth Whitening

Professional in-clinic whitening for a brighter, healthier-looking smile in a single visit.

Specialty Cosmetic
Duration Single visit, 60–90 minutes
Pain expectation Painless. Some patients experience mild, short-lived tooth sensitivity afterwards.
Specialist Dr. Deepika Mod

When you might need this

  • Yellowing or discoloration from food, drink, or age
  • Tobacco or coffee staining
  • Before a special occasion
  • As the first step of a larger cosmetic plan

What the procedure involves

  1. Examination to confirm suitability (whitening only works on natural teeth, not crowns/fillings)
  2. Cleaning and pre-whitening preparation
  3. Application of professional-grade whitening gel
  4. Activation with a curing light
  5. Multiple applications during the same visit for maximum effect

How long it takes

Single visit, 60–90 minutes.

Pain expectation

Painless. Some patients experience mild, short-lived tooth sensitivity afterwards.

Technology used

  • Professional-grade whitening gel (higher concentration than over-the-counter)
  • Curing light for activation
  • Gum protection during treatment

Recovery & aftercare

No downtime. Avoid heavily staining foods and drinks (coffee, red wine, tobacco) for 48 hours after treatment.
