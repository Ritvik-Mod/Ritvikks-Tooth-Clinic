Resources · Before your visit

New patient
guide.

Everything you need to know before your first visit to Ritvikk’s Tooth Clinic.

Before your visit

What to bring

  • Previous dental X-rays or scans, if you have them (digital files or printed images both work)
  • A list of your medications, especially blood thinners, diabetes medications, or anything you take regularly
  • Any specific questions or concerns — write them down so you remember them in the chair

Before you arrive

  • Eat a normal meal, unless your appointment is for sedation
  • Brush and floss as usual
  • Plan to arrive 10 minutes early to fill out forms
  • Bring a friend or family member if you would feel more comfortable

What to expect at your first visit

Step 1 · Medical history and discussion

You will complete a short medical history form and discuss your main concerns with Dr. Mod. This is the time to ask questions.

Step 2 · Examination

A thorough oral examination — under magnification when needed — to assess your teeth, gums, bite, and any specific concerns.

Step 3 · Diagnostic imaging

Digital X-rays (RVG) are taken as needed. These use a fraction of the radiation of traditional film X-rays.

Step 4 · Treatment plan

You receive a written treatment plan with all options — including the option to do nothing — and clear cost estimates. We never start treatment without your understanding and consent.

Payment

We accept cash and UPI. Bank transfer is available on request.

Privacy and comfort

The clinic operates by appointment, never overbooked. You will not be rushed. We allocate time for questions and explanation as part of every visit.

Running late or need to reschedule? Please let us know on WhatsApp as soon as possible. We hold your slot — please be considerate of other patients.
