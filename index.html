Our specialty · Microendodontics

What the naked eye misses, a microscope finds.

Microendodontics is root-canal treatment performed under a high-powered dental operating microscope. The result: higher first-time success, fewer re-treatments, and outcomes that last decades — not years.

Magnification Up to 25×
First-time success ~95% with microscope
Sitting time 45–90 min · 1–2 visits
Recovery Same-day return to work

Our technology

Built around
the operating microscope.

International-standard equipment, calibrated and sterilised to the same protocols you'd expect from a teaching hospital.

Operating microscope · 8×–25×

The microscope changes what's possible in endodontics.

Enhanced magnification reveals canal anatomy invisible to the unaided eye — second canals, calcified pathways, microscopic fractures. The same clarity that lets us find problems lets us treat them precisely.

Common questions

Before you
book — read these.

With modern anesthesia and microscope-assisted technique, root canal treatment is largely painless during the procedure. Mild soreness for 1–2 days afterwards is normal and easily managed with simple medication.

Most root canals are completed in one or two sittings of 45–90 minutes each, depending on the tooth and complexity. You'll receive a clear estimate after the first examination.

The fastest way is WhatsApp or a direct phone call. You can also use the form on our contact page and we'll respond the same day.

Call us immediately at +91 90160 71487. Our Dental Emergency Guide covers what to do for common urgent situations.

Your first visit is typically examination, diagnosis and a written treatment plan. We explain everything before proceeding. See the New Patient Guide for what to expect.
