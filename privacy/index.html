Legal · Privacy

Privacy notice

How we handle the information you share with us.

This notice explains how Ritvikk’s Tooth Clinic (“we”, “us”, “the clinic”) handles personal information collected through this website.

What we collect

  • Information you submit through forms — name, phone number, email (if provided), reason for visit, and any notes you add to the appointment request form.
  • Information you share via WhatsApp or phone — when you contact us, we may store the conversation history and any details you choose to share.
  • Anonymous traffic information — basic statistics about page visits (no individual tracking).

What we do not collect

  • We do not use third-party advertising trackers.
  • We do not sell or share your information with marketers.
  • We do not store payment information on the website.

How we use it

  • To respond to your appointment request or enquiry.
  • To maintain a clinical record once you become a patient (handled separately under standard medical record-keeping practice).
  • To send appointment reminders or follow-ups if you have asked us to.

How we store it

Form submissions are processed through Web3Forms and forwarded to the clinic’s email. Clinical records are stored within the clinic’s standard record-keeping system and treated as confidential medical information.

Your rights

  • Ask for a copy of the information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct anything that is wrong.
  • Ask us to delete non-clinical information at any time. Clinical records may need to be retained under medical record-keeping rules.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us.

Cookies

This website uses minimal functional cookies only — no advertising or analytics cookies that track you across other sites.

Third-party services

  • Web3Forms — handles form submissions.
  • Google Maps — for the location map on the contact page.
  • WhatsApp — for the chat link.

These services have their own privacy policies, which apply when you interact with them.

Updates

If this notice changes substantially, the updated date will be reflected here.
